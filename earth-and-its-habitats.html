V. Define

1. Landslide
A mass of rocks and soils that slides down a slope. The vibration from the earthquake cause these to happen.
2. Internal structure
Materials that make up the earth. Internal means inside so we are referring to materials inside the earth that we can’t see.
3. External structure
Materials that make up the surface of the earth and other thing. External means out. So we are referring to materials on the surface of the earth that we can see.

VI. Answer the following

1. What is the shape of the earth?

The earth is in sphere in shape like a ball and flattened at the north and south pole and it is called a geoid, which means earth shaped.

2. Name the different layers found in internal structure of the earth along with materials each layer consists of.

The structure of the earth is different.

  1. Crust – The crust is the thin outer layer of earth where we live. It consists of rocks.
  2. Mantle – The layer of the earth below the crust which consists of magma.
  3. Core – It the center of the earth. It consists of metals.

3. What is an epicentre?

An epicentre is the point on the earth’s surface where the most earthquake damage occurs.

4. List three types of damage that result from earthquake?

The three of damage that result from earthquake are

  1. Building collapse.
  2. Landslides and mud slides.
  3. Railway line bent.

5. What is habitat?

A habitat is a place in nature. Plants and animals live in habitat. All living thing need food, water and air to survive. Animals and behaviour are suited to different habitat.
